SecureDNA · Managing Director · Full job description

Managing Director

Turn a proven DNA-screening platform into global infrastructure.

The challenge

Most gene-synthesis providers still don't screen their orders. We know from red-teaming that if you order fragments of smallpox, Ebola, or 1918 influenza, several providers will ship them to you. This is solvable, and the target is clear: within five years, get 80%+ of the world's DNA providers screening against 99%+ of hazardous sequences, with EU and US mandates plausibly covering a significant portion of the market by the end of 2026.

About SecureDNA

SecureDNA is a nonprofit foundation building free, privacy-preserving screening infrastructure for DNA synthesis — one of the most critical parts of global biosecurity. Our platform screens orders automatically and in real time, free of charge, built on cryptography that keeps both the customer's order and the underlying hazard database confidential. It is live, stable, and among the most widely used screening systems in the world. Even so, much of global synthesis still goes unscreened — and driving that adoption is the job.

Our team

We are a small, senior, and globally distributed team that operates with unusual leverage. Each person owns meaningful scope and works directly on the problems that matter, rather than managing layers beneath them. This role is central to keeping it effective as we grow.

What you'll own

Strategy & direction

  • Set SecureDNA's biosecurity direction and domain strategy, leading in close collaboration with the Strategic Partnerships & Client Relationships Director and the council board.

Team & operations

  • Build the operating backbone — systems, processes, tooling, and vendor/contractor relationships — that lets a small, distributed team scale. A hands-on role for someone with skin in the game, not a delegation mentality.
  • Own hiring and people operations across full-time employees and contractors.
  • Manage, mentor, and support existing staff, including the Technical Director and the Strategic Partnerships & Client Relationships Director.
  • Own SecureDNA's long- and short-term financial sustainability, including funding strategy and financial control.
  • Administer the Swiss foundation: finance, bookkeeping, annual report and filings, compliance, and entity administration across jurisdictions, with vendor and council support.

Growth & external relationships

  • Empower and resource the Strategic Partnerships & Client Relationships Director to build relationships across synthesis providers, integrators, industry bodies, and funders.
  • Support policy and government-affairs engagements.
  • Drive adoption by unblocking and resourcing customer success and marketing.

Positioning & narrative

  • Shape SecureDNA's external positioning and the story it tells the world.

Who we're looking for

Above all, an owner rather than an administrator — someone who judges their own success by the bottom line: the prevalence of DNA screening, and a world in which malicious actors have fewer pathways to success. We can't name a single background that prepares someone for this, so instead we've listed signals that might predict fit. No one will have all of them; one or more is a strong sign.

  • Built and led a team from scratch.
  • Worked with or across different regulatory regimes.
  • Operated at the intersection of policy and science.
  • Built a standard, coalition, or shared resource that independent — even competing — organizations chose to adopt.
  • Enough scientific fluency to go deep on the substance yourself (biosecurity or life-sciences expertise isn't required, but the ability to engage the science is an advantage).
  • Been a credible public voice for a cause or company.
  • Worked closely with multiple stakeholder types: businesses, industry groups, regulators, and policymakers.

Remote, based anywhere · Regular travel to Europe & Asia · $180,000–250,000
