Managing Director

Lead the drive to make DNA screening universal, and cut off a major path to an engineered pandemic.

Making DNA to order has become fast, cheap, and widely available, enabling significant progress in medicine, agriculture, and environmental research. But serious risks accompany this progress. It's been proven that, without adequate screening, synthesis providers could unwittingly supply the genetic material needed to reconstruct pathogens like smallpox, Ebola, or 1918 influenza.

This problem is unusual in being enormously important yet solvable. If we can ensure that a large percentage of the synthetic nucleic acids produced worldwide are screened for hazards, without disrupting the industry or the vital research it supports, we would have closed off one of the most important pathways available to anyone seeking to cause a deliberate pandemic.

SecureDNA has built a sophisticated, sensitive, and cryptographically-secure screening software that lets providers screen every order in real time, without exposing their customers' data or the underlying hazard database. It's free for standard synthesis providers and other entities with meaningful capabilities of custom nucleic acid production, and since the system's launch in 2024, is already one of the most widely used screening systems in the world.

Our goal now is to drive adoption by working with both industry and policymakers: within five years, get 80%+ of the world's synthetic DNA screened before synthesis, with incoming EU and US mandates plausibly covering a significant portion of the market by the end of 2026.

We've proven the platform and the model, and are now looking for the right person to help scale it into global infrastructure.
